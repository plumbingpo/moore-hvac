Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Norman

Most mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Norman

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
