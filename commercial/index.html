Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and customers notice the drafts and the heat right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by service logs and written reports after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Moore HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Moore and surrounding areas. Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Our commercial maintenance program gets built around your equipment, how the space is used, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes matched to the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand. Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
